\section{Supervised Fine-Tuning}
\label{sec:sft}

\subsection{Model Selection and Setup}
\label{sec:sft:model}

We evaluated Llama~3.1~8B~\cite{roziere2024codellama} and Qwen3-8B~\cite{qwen2025qwen3} in 4-bit quantized form. Qwen3-8B achieves a substantially higher zero-shot baseline (70.7\% vs.\ 37.5\%), and Llama showed regression on numeric questions after SFT ($-1.4$pp), so we selected Qwen3-8B. We disabled Qwen3's thinking mode during SFT because the exploratory thinking traces conflicted with our polished chain-of-thought training data, causing overfitting to shallow pattern-matched answers.

We use 4-bit QLoRA~\cite{dettmers2023qlora} via Unsloth with LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} ($r\!=\!16$, $\alpha\!=\!32$, dropout 0.05) on all attention and MLP layers. Key training hyperparameters: LR=$2\!\times\!10^{-4}$ with cosine schedule, AdamW 8-bit optimizer, effective batch size 8, NEFTune~\cite{jain2023neftune} $\alpha\!=\!5$, max 8 epochs with early stopping (patience 2, consistently converging to epoch 4). Training examples follow a three-turn chat format (system/user/assistant) with chain-of-thought reasoning.

\subsection{Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:sft:eval}

We use $k$-fold cross-validation (5-fold for $N\!\leq\!501$, 10-fold for $N\!=\!866$) with a fresh model per fold. All evaluation uses greedy decoding. Answers are extracted via pattern matching and compared against ground truth through exact match, numerical match (5\% tolerance), fraction/percentage normalization, and substring containment. Statistical significance is assessed via the Wilcoxon signed-rank test ($p < 0.05$).

\subsection{SFT Results}
\label{sec:sft:results}

Table~\ref{tab:sft_results} presents SFT results across dataset sizes. Improvement scales non-linearly: marginal below 300 examples, emerging at 501 ($+6.2\%$, $p=0.063$), and significant at 866 ($+18.6\%$, $p=0.002$). The question flip ratio improves from 1.3:1 (215 samples: 23 W$\to$R vs.\ 18 R$\to$W) to 4.6:1 (866 samples: 206 W$\to$R vs.\ 45 R$\to$W), confirming that larger augmented datasets produce more robust improvements.

\subsection{Ablation Summary}
\label{sec:sft:ablation}

On the 215-question dataset (5-fold CV), LR=$2\!\times\!10^{-4}$ consistently outperforms $1\!\times\!10^{-4}$ ($+2.3\%$ vs.\ $-0.5\%$); NEFTune $\alpha\!=\!7$ degrades performance ($-1.3\%$); and both DPO ($-1.7\%$) and early GRPO ($+0.5\%$) underperform pure SFT. Early stopping (patience 2) consistently selects epoch 4 across all dataset sizes. Full ablation details are in Appendix~A. Note that the early GRPO result used TRL with LLM-judge rewards on 215 questions; our improved pipeline (Section~\ref{sec:grpo}) achieves $+7.5$pp with verifiable rewards on 266 mixed-difficulty questions.
